Common questions

How do I add staff? Settings → Cloud sync and team → Turn on cloud sync → Invite staff, then send them the code.

How do I move to a new phone? Install Rentix, tap “Sign in / Join with a code”, sign in and connect your workspace.

How do I delete my account? Settings → Cloud sync and team → Delete account.
