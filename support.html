Rentix – Support

Rentix is an app by Sitiyo (Panama). Need help? Email Rentix support at support@mibrocha.com and we will reply as soon as possible.

Common questions

How do I add staff? Settings → Cloud sync and team → Turn on cloud sync → Invite staff, then send them the code.

How do I move to a new phone? Install Rentix, tap “I already have an account” and sign in with the same email (or Sign in with Apple). Your business comes back automatically. Forgot the password? Tap “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen.

How do I join my boss’s team? Ask the owner for an invite code, then tap “Join a team with a code”.

How do I delete my account? Settings → Cloud sync and team → Delete account. Deleting the account does not cancel Rentix Pro: cancel the subscription in your App Store or Google Play settings.
