Rentix – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Rentix is published by David Levi (Panama). Contact: nikoslevi18@gmail.com.

Data stored on your phone

Your items, customers, bookings, expenses, photos and signatures are stored on your device. If you do not create an account, none of it is sent to us.

Optional account and cloud sync

If you create an account and turn on cloud sync, we store: your email address; your business data (items, customers, bookings, payments, expenses, contract signatures, settings); and the photos you attach. This data is stored on Supabase servers and can be read only by you and the people you invite to your workspace (your staff). Invited staff can see and edit the same business data.

Deleting your data

You can turn sync off at any time. In the app, Settings → Cloud sync and team → Delete account permanently deletes your account, and your workspace and its photos if nobody else belongs to it. You can also email us to request deletion.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google and by RevenueCat. We receive your subscription status, not your payment details.

Permissions

Camera and photo library (to add pictures to items and bookings) and notifications (reminders you turn on). Without sync, photos stay on your device.

Your customers’ data

The names and phone numbers of your customers are yours. You are responsible for handling them according to the law in your country. We do not use them for anything.

What we do not do

No advertising, no tracking, no selling or sharing of data with third parties other than the service providers needed to run the app (Supabase for storage, Apple/Google/RevenueCat for subscriptions).

Children

Rentix is a business tool and is not directed to children.

Contact and rights

Email nikoslevi18@gmail.com to ask about, correct or delete your data.
